Questions for the Speedup shorts expert. Answer directly under each
question, right in this file, and send it back. Where a question offers
options, deleting the ones that do not apply works great too.

One thing to know before answering: the tool reads what sellers type into
their listings. Sellers write things inconsistently, so the more variants
you give me, the fewer good listings we miss.
